Welcome — here's what's available

You're set up and monitoring your fleet. Everything below is optional and off by default — this won't show again, but you can reopen it anytime from Settings → General.
Ham radio integrations
QRZ / RadioID lookupShow the active caller's name and location, not just their callsign
APRS + APRS messagingLive position lookups, plus alerts when a favorite comes on the air
PSK ReporterSee where your own signal was actually heard
WSJT-X live loggingPlot each FT8/FT4 QSO on the map the instant you log it
Extra dashboard cards
Fleet activityA chart of your fleet's call activity over time
HF Conditions / Band ActivitySolar propagation data and live WSPR band activity
Big Ass ClockDigital, analog, or TIX-style clock with a second timezone
License QuizOne random Technician/General/Extra practice question, rotating
Live map overlays
Grey line / Aurora ovalDay-night terminator and NOAA auroral activity
POTA spots / PSK ReporterCurrent park activations and your own propagation reach
Cameras
RTSP / Wyze / Bambu A1Live MJPEG camera feeds alongside your hotspot cards
